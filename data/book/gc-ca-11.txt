| Year | Named by the United Nations for |

- The years 2021 to 2030 are the UN Decade on Ecosystem Restoration.

## 🎯 Themes worth knowing
- **Republic Day 2026:** 150 years of Vande Mataram.
- **World Environment Day 2025:** ending plastic pollution; host, the Republic of Korea.
- **International Year of Cooperatives 2025:** 'Cooperatives Build a Better World'.
- Themes change every year: learn the newest one just before the examination.

! trick | Trick: the teachers' three
**5 September, Teachers' Day in India; 5 October, World Teachers' Day; 8 September, Literacy Day.**

! exam | Exam favourites
- National Science Day is 28 February.
- The International Day of Yoga is 21 June.
- National Education Day, 11 November, is the birthday of Maulana Azad.

## 👆 Quick recall
? On which date is International Literacy Day observed? :: 8 September
? Whose birthday is National Mathematics Day? :: Srinivasa Ramanujan
? Which day is observed on 26 December? :: Veer Bal Diwas
? Which year was the International Year of Millets? :: 2023
