# Languages, literature and authors
> India speaks in many tongues, and each has given the country a shelf of great books. In the exam the question is almost always the same: **who wrote what, and in which language?**

## 🗣️ Languages
- The Constitution lists 22 languages in the Eighth Schedule. Hindi in the Devanagari script is the official language of the Union.
- Most languages of the north belong to the Indo-Aryan family; those of the south (Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam) to the Dravidian family.
- Sanskrit grammar was fixed by **Panini** in the 'Ashtadhyayi'; Patanjali wrote the 'Mahabhashya' on it.
- Pali was the language of the early Buddhist books, Prakrit of the Jain books.
- **Classical languages**: Tamil was the first (2004), then Sanskrit, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam and Odia. Marathi, Pali, Prakrit, Assamese and Bengali were added in 2024, making eleven.

## 📖 Modern Hindi literature
| Period | Writers |
| Bharatendu age | Bharatendu Harishchandra, the father of modern Hindi literature |
| Dwivedi age | Mahavir Prasad Dwivedi; Maithili Sharan Gupt ('Saket') |
| Chhayavad | Jaishankar Prasad ('Kamayani'), Sumitranandan Pant, Suryakant Tripathi 'Nirala', Mahadevi Varma |
| Fiction | Munshi Premchand ('Godan', 'Gaban'); Phanishwar Nath Renu ('Maila Anchal') |
| Later poets | Ramdhari Singh 'Dinkar' ('Rashmirathi'); Harivansh Rai Bachchan ('Madhushala') |

- Premchand was born at Lamhi near Varanasi; Jaishankar Prasad too belonged to Varanasi.

- The Jnanpith Award is the highest literary award of India; the Sahitya Akademi gives awards in 24 languages.

! trick | Trick: three books in Awadhi and Braj
**Jayasi's Padmavat and Tulsi's Manas are in Awadhi; Sur's Sur Sagar is in Braj.**

! exam | Exam favourites
- The 'Ashtadhyayi' of Panini is a book of Sanskrit grammar.
- Tamil was the first classical language of India (2004).
- 'Kamayani' was written by Jaishankar Prasad; 'Godan' by Premchand.

## 👆 Quick recall
? Who wrote the 'Rajatarangini'? :: Kalhana
? In which language is the 'Ramcharitmanas' written? :: Awadhi
? Who is called the father of modern Hindi literature? :: Bharatendu Harishchandra
? Which novel contains the song 'Vande Mataram'? :: Anandamath

! recap | 30-second recap
- Sanskrit: Kalidasa, Banabhatta, Kalhana, Jayadeva.
- Medieval: Tulsidas, Surdas, Kabir, Jayasi; Persian histories of the Mughals.
- Modern Hindi: Bharatendu, Premchand and the four poets of Chhayavad.

# भाषा, साहित्य एवं रचनाकार
> भारत अनेक भाषाओं में बोलता है, और हर भाषा ने देश को महान ग्रंथों की एक पूरी अलमारी दी है। परीक्षा में प्रश्न लगभग सदा एक ही होता है: **किसने क्या लिखा, और किस भाषा में?**

## 🗣️ भाषाएँ
- संविधान की आठवीं अनुसूची में 22 भाषाएँ हैं। देवनागरी लिपि में हिंदी संघ की राजभाषा है।
- उत्तर की अधिकांश भाषाएँ भारतीय-आर्य परिवार की हैं; दक्षिण की (तमिल, तेलुगु, कन्नड़, मलयालम) द्रविड़ परिवार की।
- संस्कृत व्याकरण को **पाणिनि** ने 'अष्टाध्यायी' में सूत्रबद्ध किया; पतंजलि ने उस पर 'महाभाष्य' लिखा।
- पालि आरंभिक बौद्ध ग्रंथों की भाषा थी, प्राकृत जैन ग्रंथों की।
- **शास्त्रीय भाषाएँ**: तमिल पहली थी (2004), फिर संस्कृत, तेलुगु, कन्नड़, मलयालम और ओड़िया। 2024 में मराठी, पालि, प्राकृत, असमिया और बांग्ला जोड़ी गईं, जिससे संख्या ग्यारह हो गई।

## 📖 आधुनिक हिंदी साहित्य
| काल | रचनाकार |

- प्रेमचंद का जन्म वाराणसी के निकट लमही में हुआ; जयशंकर प्रसाद भी वाराणसी के थे।
