# Unit-1: Algebra
## Relations and functions
- Types of relations: reflexive, symmetric, transitive and equivalence relations; equivalence classes
- One-one and onto functions; composite of functions; inverse of a function
- Binary operations
## Matrices
- Types of matrices; transpose of a matrix; symmetric and skew-symmetric matrices
- Addition and multiplication of matrices
- Singular and non-singular matrices; invertible matrices
## Determinants
- Properties of determinants
- Adjoint and inverse of a square matrix
- Consistency and number of solutions of a system of linear equations; solving systems of linear equations in two or three variables
## Equations and series
- Theory of equations of degree two or more
- Arithmetic, geometric and harmonic progressions
- Permutations and combinations; binomial theorem
- Exponential and logarithmic series
## Probability
- Multiplication theorem of probability; conditional probability
- Independent events; total probability
# Unit-2: Calculus
## Functions of one variable
- Limit of a function; continuity and differentiability of a function
- Derivative of a composite function
- Rolle's theorem; Lagrange's and Cauchy's mean value theorems
- Maclaurin's and Taylor's series; L'Hospital's rule
- Successive differentiation; Leibnitz's theorem
- Equations of the tangent and the normal to a given curve
- Maxima and minima; increasing and decreasing functions
## Integration
- Methods of integration
- Definite integral as the limit of a sum; basic properties and evaluation of definite integrals
- Applications in finding the area under simple curves
- Riemann integration
# Unit-3: Ordinary Differential Equations
- Order and degree of differential equations
- Formation of differential equations
- Solution of differential equations of the first order and first degree
- Singular solutions
- Linear differential equations with constant coefficients
- Homogeneous differential equations
# Unit-4: Coordinate Geometry of Two Dimensions
- General equation of the second degree in two variables (x, y) and its classification into a pair of straight lines, circle, parabola, ellipse and hyperbola
- Equations of tangents and normals
- Common tangents to two conics
- Pair of tangents and chord of contact
# Unit-5: Vectors and Three-Dimensional Geometry
## Vectors
- Vectors and scalars; unit vectors; direction cosines and direction ratios of a vector
- Multiplication of a vector by a scalar
- Dot product and cross product of vectors and their simple applications
## Three-dimensional geometry
- Direction cosines and direction ratios of the line joining two points
- Cartesian and vector equations of a straight line; coplanar and skew lines; shortest distance between two lines
- Cartesian and vector equations of a plane
- Angle between (a) two lines, (b) two planes, (c) a line and a plane
- Distance of a point from a plane
- Intersection of two lines, of a line and a plane, and of two planes
- Equations of the sphere, the cone and the cylinder
# Unit-6: Abstract Algebra
- Groups and their examples; subgroups
- Homomorphism and isomorphism; properties of homomorphisms
- Order of an element in a group; cyclic groups
- Symmetric group Sₙ
- Lagrange's theorem; Fermat's theorem and its application
- Normal subgroups; fundamental theorem of homomorphism
- Rings and fields, with examples
# Unit-7: Linear Algebra
- Vector spaces with examples; subspaces
- Linear dependence and independence; basis and dimension of a vector space
- Quotient space; linear sum and direct sum of subspaces
- Linear transformations; kernel and image of a linear transformation
- Rank and nullity of a linear transformation; rank–nullity theorem
- Composite of linear transformations; singular and non-singular linear transformations
- Transpose of a linear transformation; matrix of a linear transformation
# Unit-8: Vector Calculus
## Vector differentiation
- Scalar and vector fields
- Gradient, divergence and curl; vector identities
- Directional derivatives
## Vector integration
- Line integral, surface integral and volume integral
- Green's theorem, Gauss's divergence theorem and Stokes' theorem, and their simple applications
# Unit-9: Mechanics
## Statics
- Equilibrium of a body under the action of three forces
- Coplanar forces; equilibrium of a body under a system of coplanar forces
- Centre of gravity
- Common catenary and its properties
- Moment of inertia
## Dynamics
- Motion of a projectile in a vertical plane under gravity
- Work, power and energy
- Radial and transverse velocity and acceleration
- Tangential and normal velocity and acceleration
- Central orbits
# Unit-10: Trigonometry
- Trigonometric equations
- Properties of triangles
- Inverse circular functions
- Heights and distances
- Complex numbers; De Moivre's theorem and its applications
