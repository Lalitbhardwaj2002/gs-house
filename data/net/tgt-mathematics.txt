# Unit-1: Commercial Mathematics
- Work and time; speed and time
- Simple interest; compound interest
- Banking and taxation
# Unit-2: Statistics
- Frequency distribution
- Pictorial representation of statistical data
- Measures of central tendency
- Measures of dispersion
- Statistics of birth and death (vital statistics)
- Index numbers
# Unit-3: Algebra
- Surds
- Polynomials and factors of second-degree polynomials; HCF of polynomials
- Linear equations in two unknowns; simultaneous equations in three unknowns
- Quadratic equations
- Logarithms
- Ratio and proportion
- Number system
- Set operations
- Definition of a determinant; minors and cofactors
- Expansion of determinants up to order 3 × 3 and properties of determinants
- Solution of simultaneous linear equations in three unknowns by Cramer's rule
- Types of matrices; addition and multiplication of matrices up to order 3 × 3
- Transpose of a matrix; symmetric and skew-symmetric matrices
- Solution of simultaneous equations in three variables using the inverse of a matrix
- Theory of equations; symmetric functions of roots
- Arithmetic, geometric and harmonic progressions
- Sums of the series of natural numbers and of their squares and cubes (the Hindi text names only the squares and cubes)
- Permutations and combinations
- Binomial theorem
- Sums of exponential and logarithmic series
# Unit-4: Probability
- Theorems of addition and multiplication
# Unit-5: Set Theory
- Rules of set algebra
- Equivalence relation
- Mapping; composition of mappings; inverse mapping
- Peano's postulates and the principle of mathematical induction
- Group and group homomorphism
- Subgroup generated by a subset; cyclic group; order of an element of a group; subgroups of a cyclic group
- Coset decomposition; Lagrange's theorem
# Unit-6: Real Analysis
- Axioms of real numbers
- Countability of sets
- Metric spaces; neighbourhood; open and closed sets
- Derived set; dense set; perfect set
- Bolzano–Weierstrass theorem and general theorems
- Real sequences; limit of a sequence; convergent and divergent sequences
- Bounded sequences; monotonic sequences
- Properties of convergent sequences
- Cauchy sequence; Cauchy's theorem on limits; Cauchy's criterion of convergence of a real sequence
- Limit and continuity of real-valued functions; left-hand and right-hand limits
- Properties of continuous functions
- Types of discontinuity
- Rolle's theorem
# Unit-7: Trigonometry
- Circular measure; trigonometric ratios of special angles
- Trigonometric ratios of sum and difference of angles, and of multiple and sub-multiple angles
- Trigonometric identities
- Trigonometric equations
- Solution of triangles; radii of the circumcircle, the incircle and the escribed circles and their properties
- General properties of inverse circular functions
# Unit-8: Complex Numbers
- Addition and multiplication of complex numbers
- De Moivre's theorem and its applications
- Heights and distances
- Exponential, circular and hyperbolic functions of a complex variable
- Separation into real and imaginary parts
# Unit-9: Geometry
- Baudhayana–Pythagoras theorem and its extension
- Circle and sector of a circle (segment of a circle in the Hindi text); arc and chord of a circle
- Tangent to a circle
- Alternate segment and its angles
- Segments of a chord and the rectangle formed by them
- Congruence of linear and plane figures (similarity in the Hindi text)
# Unit-10: Coordinate Geometry
- Cartesian plane (the Hindi text adds the straight line)
- Homogeneous equation of the second degree and pair of straight lines; equation of the bisectors and the angle between them
- Standard equations of conics (circle, parabola, ellipse and hyperbola) in rectangular Cartesian coordinates, and parametric equations
- Conditions for the general equation of the second degree to represent a pair of straight lines, a circle, a parabola, an ellipse and a hyperbola (the ellipse is named in the Hindi text only)
- Equations of the circle, parabola, ellipse and hyperbola using transformation (shift of origin and rotation of axes)
- Equations of the tangent and the normal to a conic
- Intersection of a line with a conic, and the condition for the line to be a tangent (limiting position)
- Parametric equation of a tangent; pair of tangents from a point to a conic
- Condition for a line to be a tangent or a normal to a conic
- Standard polar equation of a conic
- Three-dimensional geometry of the sphere, the cone and the cylinder
# Unit-11: Calculus
## Differentiation
- Differentiation of algebraic, trigonometric, exponential and logarithmic functions (the Hindi text also names the definition of differentiation)
- Tangent and normal
- Maxima and minima of a function of one variable
- Tracing of simple curves
## Integration
- Integration by parts and by substitution; integration using partial fractions
- Definite integrals and their applications in finding the area between curves, and the volumes and surfaces of the cylinder, the cone and the sphere
- Order and degree of a differential equation
- General solution of ordinary differential equations of the first order and first degree
# Unit-12: Vector Analysis
- Position vectors of an ordered pair and an ordered triad; displacement vector; free vector; unit vector
- Modulus and direction cosines
- Composition of vectors (force, velocity and acceleration); difference of two vectors; relative velocity
- Scalar and vector products of two vectors and their applications to work done and the moment of a force
- Triple products of vectors
# Unit-13: Statics
- Equilibrium of a body under three forces; Lami's theorem; triangle law of forces
- Trigonometrical theorem; resolution into two perpendicular components
- General conditions of equilibrium
- Centre of gravity
- Common catenary
# Unit-14: Dynamics
- Projectile motion
- Motion of a particle in a plane; radial and transverse velocity and acceleration
- Tangential and normal velocity and acceleration
> Official syllabus of the Uttar Pradesh Education Service Selection Commission for TGT Mathematics (subject code 07), effective for examinations from 2026. The headings, items and their order follow the official English text; a few misprints in it are corrected, and brackets note the main places where the official Hindi text differs.
