# tra-teaching — checker change log

Checked 40 draft questions (`data/draft/tra-teaching.json`; the owner capped the section at 40): **40 kept, 17 corrected, 0 deleted**. Ids are unchanged (001–040). Answer keys: A–D = 10/10/10/10. Levels: TGT 10, PGT 16, Higher 14.

I solved every key blind first, and each one matched the draft and the standard UGC-NET answer. The books have no material on this topic, so I checked the facts against these sources:
- **UGC-NET Education (Code 09) syllabus.** Unit 7 names the three levels of teaching: Memory (Herbartian model), Understanding (Morrison model) and Reflective (Bigge and Hunt model). It also names Anderson–Krathwohl, Krathwohl and R.H. Dave for the three domains, Flanders' interaction analysis and Knowles' andragogy. Unit 8 covers Gagné's nine events and Bruner, Ausubel, Vygotsky and Piaget.
- **UGC MOOCs (INFLIBNET) lecture text on micro-teaching.** It confirms Stanford 1963, one skill at a time and 5–10 pupils.
- **The 36-minute Indian cycle (6-6-12-6-6).** This follows B.K. Passi (1976), and a college of education study text confirms it. The UGC MOOC text gives "about 35 minutes" per skill, which is consistent.
- **Memory = "thoughtless" (Bigge's continuum).** A B.Ed. study text confirms this.
