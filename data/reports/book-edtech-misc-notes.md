# Educational technology and Miscellaneous notes of the book companion: fact-check of 13 November 2026

Checker R went through all thirteen chapters (gc-edt-01 to 05, gc-misc-01 to 08). Every chapter still has the same structure in English and Hindi: the structure check prints "same" for all 13 files.

## gc-edt-01 Basic concepts and objectives
- No errors found. Checked: Adams (bipolar), Dewey (tripolar), Bloom 1956, Krathwohl, Simpson/Dave/Harrow, and Anderson and Krathwohl 2001.

## gc-edt-02 Phases, levels and models
- No errors found. Checked: Jackson's phases, Herbart/Morrison/Hunt, Joyce and Weil's four families, Kilpatrick, Armstrong, Parkhurst, Skinner, Crowder and Kolb.
- Note: Jackson (1968) named the pre-active and interactive phases. Exam books usually credit him with the post-active phase too, so the chapter keeps that.

## gc-edt-03 Instructional design and micro-teaching
- Fact: the five lesson steps came from Herbart's followers (Ziller and Rein), not from Herbart himself. The line now says "Herbartian five steps, set out by Herbart's followers" (EN and HI).
- Hindi: "अदा" was changed to the standard term "आदा (इनपुट) … प्रदा (आउटपुट)".
- Checked and correct: Dwight Allen, Stanford, 1963; Flanders 7+2+1; NCTE in force 1995; NCERT 1961; Dale 1946.

## gc-edt-04 Communication and digital learning
- No errors found. Checked: Delhi school TV 1961; SITE 1975–76 (about 2,400 villages, six States); Countrywide Classroom 1984; CLASS 1984–85; Gyan Darshan 2000; Gyan Vani 2001; EDUSAT September 2004; SWAYAM's four quadrants; PM eVIDYA 2020.

## gc-edt-05 Measurement and evaluation
- Hindi: "परिवेश प्रभाव" was changed to "प्रभामंडल प्रभाव" for the halo effect, and "स्थापन" to the usual "स्थानन" for placement evaluation.

## gc-misc-02 Political thought

## gc-misc-04 Indian education schemes and institutions
- No errors found. Checked: the years of all the schemes and institutions, the five RIEs, Bharat Scouts and Guides 1950, and that three-fourths of SMC members are parents.

## gc-misc-05 Research concepts
- Hindi: pragmatism was given as "व्यवहारवाद", which means behaviourism. It is now "प्रयोजनवाद".

## gc-misc-06 Hypothesis, data, sampling
- No errors found. Checked: Type I and Type II errors, Moreno, Likert and the sampling types.

## gc-misc-07 Sports
- Fact: "love all" was described as being called at the start of the game in all three sports. It now says that "love all" means both sides are at zero and that the term is used in badminton, tennis and table tennis.
- Out of date: the Federation Cup (football) has not been held since 2017. The line now says "held from 1977 to 2017".
- Checked and correct: trophies, team sizes, Arjuna 1961, Dronacharya 1985, Athens 1896, Asian Games 1951, Bindra 2008, and the book authors.

## gc-misc-08 Uttar Pradesh
- Fact: the State's first floating solar plant to produce power is NTPC's 20 MW plant at Dibiyapur, Auraiya (September 2022). The 150 MW Rihand (Sonbhadra) project was only approved in 2019. Both are now given.
- Fact: Nautanki is folk theatre, not a folk dance, and was moved out of the list of dances.
- Fact: UNESCO's 2017 listing covers the Kumbh Mela as a whole, not only Prayagraj's. The line was reworded and the year added.
- Could go out of date: "75 districts and 18 divisions" is now marked "(2026)", and the crops in which UP ranks first are now introduced with "in recent years".
- Checked and correct: UP Diwas (24 January 1950, first held 2018), State symbols, institutions and their places, Jaunpur as the 'Shiraz of India', Sonbhadra's tribal population, ODOP (24 January 2018) and the town–product pairs.
