# General Science and Technology notes of the book companion: fact-check of 13 November 2026

## gc-sci-01 Measurement, motion, force and energy
No errors found. Checked SI base units, instruments, Newton's laws and examples, friction order, Cavendish (1798), g, escape velocity, work/energy/power units, 1 hp = 746 W, 1 kWh = 3.6 × 10⁶ J.

## gc-sci-02 Fluids, heat and sound
- Hindi only: "ताप गर्माहट की मात्रा है" could be read as "quantity of heat"; now "ताप किसी वस्तु के गर्म होने की कोटि (स्तर) है", matching "degree of hotness".
Also checked Pascal, Archimedes, Bernoulli, temperature scales, −40°, 4°C, speed of sound, frequency ranges, 17 m echo, Mach number.

## gc-sci-03 Light and optics
- Hindi only: the VIBGYOR order was missing in Hindi; added "(बैंजनीहपीनाला)".
Also checked mirrors and uses, refractive indices, dioptre, total internal reflection, scattering, eye parts and defects.

## gc-sci-04 Electricity, electronics and magnetism
- Mains supply said only "220 volts". Now "220 volts (230 V by the present standard), 50 hertz", both languages.
Also checked units and meters, Ohm's law, resistance rules, materials of heater/filament/fuse, Oersted 1820, Faraday 1831, Fleming's rules, transistor 1947, Kilby 1958, Onnes 1911.

## gc-sci-05 Atoms, nuclei, the universe and inventions
- The Chandrasekhar limit was applied to the mass of the whole star, which is misleading. Now: a dying star becomes a white dwarf if its leftover core is below the limit (about 1.44 solar masses); heavier cores become neutron stars or black holes. Both languages.
Also checked the discovery timeline (1895–1938), alpha/beta/gamma, fission and fusion, reactor parts, Einstein's 1921 Nobel, light year, parsec, planets, Pluto (2006), inventors.

## gc-sci-06 Matter, atoms and the periodic table
No errors found. Checked states of matter, separation methods, discoverers of particles, atomic models, isotopes and isobars, Avogadro's number, periodic table history (1817, 1865, 1869, 1913), 18 groups / 7 periods / 118 elements, element records.

## gc-sci-07 Chemical reactions, acids, bases, salts and water
- The table gave formic acid as the acid of "ants and bees". Bee venom is not formic acid; now "Ant sting; nettle leaves" (Hindi: चींटी का डंक; बिच्छू बूटी (नेटल) की पत्तियाँ).
Also checked reaction types, natural acids, pH values, acid rain 5.6, aqua regia 3:1, chemical names of common salts, hardness of water.

## gc-sci-08 Carbon, fuels, metals and industrial chemistry
- Fullerene was described as always sixty carbon atoms; now "commonly sixty" (C60 is the best known; others such as C70 exist). Both languages.
Also checked allotropes, fuel gases, coal grades, ethyl mercaptan, alloys, ores, Haber and Contact processes, urea 46% N, uses of gases.

## gc-sci-09 The living world
No errors found. Checked branches of biology, fathers of biology/botany/taxonomy, ranks, Whittaker 1969, Woese 1990, five kingdoms, microbes, plant groups, animal phyla, heart chambers.

## gc-sci-10 Cell, tissues and biomolecules
- The table said the cell wall is "found in plant cells only" (fungi and bacteria also have walls). Now "Made of cellulose in plants; absent in animal cells". Both languages.
Also checked Hooke 1665, Leeuwenhoek 1674, Brown 1831, Schleiden and Schwann 1838, Virchow 1855, organelles, mitosis and meiosis, tissues, Watson and Crick 1953.

## gc-sci-11 Plant life
No errors found. Checked modified roots/stems/leaves, photosynthesis (magnesium, oxygen from water, C4 plants), transpiration, guttation, plant hormones, tropisms, vegetative and asexual reproduction, double fertilisation, parthenocarpy.

## gc-sci-12 Genetics, evolution and ecology
No errors found. Checked Mendel (1866) and his laws and ratios, incomplete dominance, sex determination, Landsteiner, genetic disorders, Lamarck 1809, Darwin 1859, de Vries 1901, Oparin 1924, Miller–Urey 1953, homologous/analogous organs, Tansley 1935, 10 per cent law, nutrient cycles.

## gc-tech-01 Digital technology, AI, robotics and quantum technology
No errors found. Checked Aadhaar (UIDAI, 12 digits), UPI (NPCI, 2016), digital rupee (still a pilot in 2026), McCarthy 1956, Turing 1950, Čapek 1920, Vyommitra, 5G launch 1 October 2022, Bitcoin 2009, India Semiconductor Mission 2021, PARAM 8000 (1991), National Supercomputing Mission 2015, National Quantum Mission 2023.

## gc-tech-02 Biotechnology and health technology
- Human insulin was dated 1983. Humulin (made with E. coli) was approved by the US FDA in October 1982; the line now says 1982 and notes that NCERT gives 1983. The recap line is changed to match. Both languages.
- GM mustard said only "cleared for environmental release in 2022". Added that the case is pending in the Supreme Court (split verdict July 2024, now before a larger bench). Both languages.
Also checked tools of genetic engineering, Bt cotton (2002, still the only GM crop grown commercially), Bt brinjal 2009/2010, GEAC, IVF 1978, Jeffreys 1984, gene therapy 1990, Dolly 1996, Human Genome Project 2003, Covaxin and Covishield, Lalji Singh, imaging technologies, eSanjeevani, ABHA.

## gc-tech-03 Space, nuclear and defence technology
- The opening said India "reached the south pole of the Moon"; Chandrayaan-3 landed near it. Now "the south polar region". Both languages.
- Hindi only: the satellite name was spelt आर्यभट्ट; now the standard आर्यभट (three places).
Also checked Thumba 1963, ISRO 15 August 1969, Aryabhata 19 April 1975, SLV-3 1980, Rakesh Sharma 1984, Chandrayaan-1, Mangalyaan 2014, Chandrayaan-3 23 August 2023, Aditya-L1, SpaDeX docking, NISAR and Shubhanshu Shukla (all 2025), rockets, orbits, NavIC, Apsara 1956, Tarapur 1969, Pokhran 1974 and 1998, DRDO 1958, IGMDP 1983, Agni-V range, INS Vikrant 2022, INS Arihant, Mission Shakti 27 March 2019.

## Not verified online

- Everything else was checked against standard references and known facts. Only the GM mustard status, the Humulin year and the digital rupee status were checked online.
