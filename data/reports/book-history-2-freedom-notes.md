# Indian culture (History chapters 12 to 15) and Freedom Movement notes of the book companion: fact-check of 13 November 2026

## gc-hist-12 (philosophy, Bhakti, Sufi, social reform)
- Annie Besant row read as if she set up the Theosophical Society. It now says she led it; it was founded in 1875 by Blavatsky and Olcott, with headquarters at Adyar.
- Prarthana Samaj was credited to M. G. Ranade alone. It now names Atmaram Pandurang (founder) with Ranade, and adds 1867, Bombay.

## gc-hist-13 (languages, literature, authors)
No errors found. Checked the eleven classical languages and their years, the Eighth Schedule, all Sanskrit, medieval and modern books with their authors and languages, Premchand and Prasad, Jnanpith and the 24 Sahitya Akademi languages.

## gc-hist-14 (architecture, sculpture, painting)
No errors found. Checked every monument and its builder, the three temple styles, the schools of sculpture and painting, Bani Thani, Bharat Mata and the folk paintings with their States.

## gc-hist-15 (music, dance, theatre, festivals, cinema)
No errors found. Checked instruments and masters, the eight classical dances, folk dances and theatre, festivals, the UNESCO list (Deepavali was inscribed on 10 December 2025), the akademi years (1952, 1954, 1954), the first films and the Phalke Award (1969).

## gc-free-01 (tribal revolts and 1857)
No errors found. Checked the years and leaders of all risings, the 1857 dates, the centres and leaders, and the results of the 1858 Act.

## gc-free-02 (Congress, partition of Bengal, Swadeshi, Home Rule)
- The Ghadar Party was said to be formed "at San Francisco". It was founded in 1913 in Oregon; San Francisco was its headquarters. It now says "with its headquarters at San Francisco".
- Also checked: Congress founding and sessions, presidents, the partition dates, Home Rule Leagues, the Lucknow Pact, the Montagu declaration.

## gc-free-03 (revolutionary movement)
- Same Ghadar wording fixed in the timeline.
- Hindi only: spelling "प्रीतिलता वाद्देदार" changed to "प्रीतिलता वादेदार".
- Also checked: Chapekar (1897), Muzaffarpur (1908), Hardinge bomb (1912), Kabul government (1915), HRA (Kanpur 1924), Kakori (9 August 1925) and the four jails (Gorakhpur, Faizabad, Gonda, Allahabad), HSRA (Delhi 1928), Assembly bombs (1929), 23 March 1931.

## gc-free-04 (Champaran to Non-Cooperation)
- The timeline said Kashi Vidyapith, Jamia Millia Islamia and Gujarat Vidyapith were all opened in 1921. Gujarat Vidyapith and Jamia opened in 1920, Kashi Vidyapith in 1921. It now reads "1920–21" with each year shown.
- The Ahmedabad strike of 1918 was called "his first fast"; Gandhi had already fasted in South Africa. It now says "his first hunger strike in India".
- Hindi only: the awkward line on the Ali brothers now reads "इसके नेता अली बंधु (मुहम्मद अली और शौकत अली) थे।"
- Also checked: return on 9 January 1915, Rowlatt, Jallianwala, Hunter Committee, Non-Cooperation dates, Chauri Chaura (5 February 1922) and Bardoli (12 February 1922).

## gc-free-05 (Simon Commission to the Poona Pact)
No errors found. Checked the Swaraj Party, Simon Commission, Nehru Report, Lahore session, Dandi March (78 followers, about 390 km), Dharasana, Gandhi–Irwin Pact (5 March 1931), Karachi, the Round Table Conferences, the Communal Award and Poona Pact, the 1935 Act.

## gc-free-06 (peasants, Bose, INA, Quit India)
No errors found. Checked the Kisan Sabhas, Eka, Bardoli 1928, AITUC, CSP, Haripura and Tripuri, Forward Bloc, INA and Azad Hind (21 October 1943), Moirang, the August Offer, Individual Satyagraha, Cripps, Quit India and the three parallel governments.

## gc-free-07 (partition, independence, slogans, books, titles)
No errors found. Checked the Shimla Conference, RIN revolt, Cabinet Mission, Direct Action Day, Interim Government (2 September 1946), Attlee's announcement (20 February 1947), the 3 June Plan, the integration of Junagadh, Hyderabad and Kashmir, and every slogan, book, paper and title.

## Not verified online
Only Deepavali on the UNESCO list was confirmed by web search. All other facts were checked against standard sources from memory. Two points where books differ and the text was left as is: the Aligarh college year (school 1875, college 1877) and the start year of the Sanyasi rebellion (1763 or 1770).
