# Polity notes of the book companion: fact-check of 13 November 2026

Files checked: data/book/gc-pol-01.txt to gc-pol-17.txt. Every changed English line was mirrored in Hindi. The section and line counts still match in all 17 files.

Totals: 9 factual corrections (each made in both English and Hindi) and 5 Hindi-only corrections.

| Chapter | Corrections | Not verified, or left as it was |
| 01 British-era Acts | None | None |
| 02 Constituent Assembly | Members from British India were elected indirectly. Members from the princely states were nominated by their rulers. The old text said all members were elected by the provincial assemblies. | None |
| 03 Structure, Preamble | None | "About 470 Articles" kept. The count depends on how lettered Articles are counted. |
| 04 Union, citizenship | Hindi only: Article 1 now quotes the official text, अर्थात् (it said अर्थात). | 28 States and 8 Union Territories confirmed as of 2026. |
| 05 Fundamental Rights | Article 24 now covers hazardous work as well as factories and mines. Article 28 now says "wholly State-funded" schools. | None |
| 06 DPSP, Duties | Article 45 covers children below 6 years. The old text said up to 6 years. | None |
| 07 Amendment | None | None |
| 08 Federal system | None | None |
| 09 Emergency | None | None |
| 10 President, VP | Hindi only: "महाभियोग सिद्ध नहीं हुआ" became "महाभियोग नहीं चलाया गया", to match the English. Hindi only: सैन्य न्यायालय became सेना न्यायालय, the term used in Article 72. | None |
| 11 PM, AG | None | None |
| 12 Parliament | The Rajya Sabha is elected by elected MLAs. The old text said "MLAs". | None |
| 13 Judiciary | Supreme Court strength is now the CJI + 37 = 38. The Supreme Court (Number of Judges) Amendment Act, 2026 replaced the May 2026 ordinance, with effect from 16 May 2026. The recap was updated too. "25 High Courts" is now dated "since 2019". | The new Ladakh bench at Leh (October 2026) is part of the High Court of Jammu & Kashmir and Ladakh. It is not a new High Court. The Justice Varma removal motion ended without a vote because he resigned in April 2026, so "no judge has been removed" still stands. |
| 14 State government | Governor and Article 161: he cannot pardon a death sentence but can commute, remit or suspend it, and he has no power over court-martial sentences. | The six States with a Legislative Council were confirmed for 2026. West Bengal has proposed a Council, but none had been created by November 2026. |
| 15 Local government | The District Planning Committee (243ZD) belongs to Part IX-A (the 74th Amendment), even though it plans for rural areas too. The table had placed it under the 73rd Amendment. | The Hindi clause letters were checked against the official sequence: 243-क, 243-झ, 243-ट, 243-ण, 243-त, 243-यघ, 243-यङ and 243-यछ. All are correct. |
| 16 Elections | "Delimitation orders cannot be challenged" was reworded. Their orders have the force of law, but courts may step in when an order is manifestly arbitrary (Kishorchandra Chhanganlal Rathod case, 23 July 2024). | "Nearly a hundred crore voters" is approximate. |
| 17 CAG and other bodies | Added a note on the 97th Amendment: in 2021 the Supreme Court (Rajendra N. Shah case) struck down Part IX-B in so far as it covers State co-operative societies. Hindi only: the SPSC row ("हटा केवल राष्ट्रपति सकता है") was reworded into natural Hindi. | None |

Sources used: Bar & Bench and AIR News on the Supreme Court judge-strength Act of 2026; Supreme Court of India judgment of 23 July 2024 and LiveLaw (delimitation); news reports on the Ladakh High Court bench and on Justice Varma (2026). All other facts were checked from standard constitutional knowledge. The official Hindi text of the Constitution was not opened online. The Hindi clause letters follow the standard sequence used in it.
