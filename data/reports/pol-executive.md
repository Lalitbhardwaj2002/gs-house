## Scope gap fixed
The draft had **no questions** on the Prime Minister, the Council of Ministers (Articles 74–78) or the Attorney General, although all three are in the section scope. Because the section is capped at 42, I replaced six questions with new ones on these topics:
- 016: was a near-duplicate of 002 and 015 (nominated members vote in the impeachment but not in the election). Replaced with a PGT statements question on Article 74: the 42nd and 44th Amendments, and 74(2), which bars courts from inquiring into the advice.
- 020: was a match of Presidents' "firsts", overlapping the notes of 014 and 019. Replaced with a PGT match of Articles 74, 75, 77 and 78 to their subjects.
- 021: was about which President proclaimed the 1975 Emergency (Emergency belongs to another section). This fact moved into 031. Replaced with a TGT question on Article 75(1A): the 15% ceiling set by the 91st Amendment, 2003.
- 023: was "which Article covers ordinances" (Article 123 is also tested by 024–026). Replaced with a TGT question on the Attorney General's right to speak in Parliament (Article 88).
- 027: was "which Article covers pardons" (Article 72 is also tested by 029). Replaced with a TGT question on collective responsibility to the Lok Sabha (Article 75(3)).
- 042: was a chronology of Vice-Presidents (a second "persons" chronology after 022). Replaced with a Higher four-statement question on the Attorney General (Article 76).

## Corrected
- 004: rewritten from a direct question into a PGT statements question on vote values (1971 Census base; uniform MP vote value; MLA vote values differ by State). A fact was corrected: the MP total is only "approximately" equal to the MLA total. Fatman p.161 now supports the vote-value formula.
- 008: note B corrected. The old note said the Speaker "administers oath to no constitutional functionary", which was vague and misleading.
- 010: a direct question labelled PGT, rewritten as a statements question in real PGT style. It covers the President's nomination (50+50), the Vice-President's (20+20), and the deposit (₹15,000 for both).
- 014: stem reworded in exam style (en + hi).
- 024: removed the NCERT reference (could not confirm that it discusses ordinances).
- 027 (old version): dropped the fact "the Governor cannot pardon a death sentence", because the Supreme Court in State of Haryana v. Raj Kumar (2021) makes it contestable. The question itself was later replaced (see above).
- 028: note A corrected. The old note named only one interchanged pair; the option swaps both pairs.
- 031: rewritten as a PGT "pairs" question with three pairs: the pocket veto of 1986 (Zail Singh), the Emergency of 1975 (Fakhruddin Ali Ahmed), and the acting President of 1977 (Jatti, not Giri). The old version was a single-fact question labelled PGT.
- 032: replaced a fact that duplicated 033 (no veto over amendment Bills) with one on Article 201. Removed the NCERT reference, which does not discuss the "qualified veto".
